<objective>
Make every count and every length field that the parse order names get checked
against the real size of the region it indexes, before any allocation is sized
from it, and prove that a new unchecked allocation cannot enter the library
without failing the gate.

Purpose: the discipline is uneven today, and the gap is located. `GuiTable::walk`
reads `VBHeader.wFormCount`, the exact field the roadmap's own success
criterion 4 names, and never compares it against what the GUI table region can
hold. It is memory safe, because every read inside the loop goes through a
bounded subregion. It is not requirement compliant: a hostile count produces a
generic damaged refusal with no byte offset of its own, not the
`ImplausibleCount` defect the criterion names, and salvage mode has nothing to
continue past.

The second half is the wall. Thirteen allocations in `crates/deform6/src/` are
sized with a capacity argument today, measured this planning session across
eight files. Five of them take a value that came out of the file. The audit is
worth doing once. A wall that fails the gate on a new one is what keeps it
done.

Output: the bound check in `gui.rs`, the audit of every capacity site with its
result recorded, and a third prove script beside the lint wall and the region
wall.
</objective>

## Trust Boundaries

| Boundary | Description |
