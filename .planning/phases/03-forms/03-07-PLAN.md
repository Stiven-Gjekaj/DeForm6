<objective>
Recover the resource blobs out of the property stream, hold their bytes, and
synthesise the offset a `.frx` writer will need.

Purpose: FRM-05 needs the resource blobs. The single most important fact about
them is that the `.frx` offset is not stored in the executable. It is a
running cursor a decompiler synthesises. A tool that computes an offset any
other way drifts, and the generated `.frm` then names a byte position the
generated `.frx` does not hold.

Output: `crates/deform6/src/vb/frx.rs`.
</objective>
